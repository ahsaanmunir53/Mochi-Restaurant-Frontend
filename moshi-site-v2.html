Our Story

Sushi that travels
like Lahore does.

MOSHI started with a stubborn question: why does sushi fall apart the moment it leaves the restaurant? The rice warms, the nori sogs, the box leaks — the best meal in the city couldn't survive Canal Road.

So instead of fighting the box, we threw it away. The MOSHI tube is a sealed, self-refrigerating cylinder that keeps a hand-rolled sushi roll at 0–4°C from our counter to your hands — no fridge, no ice pack, no hurry.

Everything inside stays uncompromised: halal-certified fish and ingredients, zero artificial preservatives, rice seasoned the same hour it's rolled. One tube, one roll. Grab it and go.
